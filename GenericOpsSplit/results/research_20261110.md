# Generic OpsSplit research comparison

Primary errors are mean trajectory relative L2, clipped at 2. Smaller ratios are better. Ratios use the strongest historical method at the same horizon. Historical comparisons are exploratory: adaptive use of the old test set requires a fresh holdout before confirmation.

A zero-parameter method is a classical solver baseline. Its improvement is numerical, not a learned gain. Different iteration counts or solver substep counts are different budgets. One-seed pilots are not confirmed winners.

Training wall times come from concurrently shared GPU runs and resumable worker chunks. They are descriptive, not controlled speed comparisons. Use the final idle-GPU holdout timings to compare inference costs; timing-completeness annotations remain in the JSON.
